Who Are We?
Career Garage is an AI-powered career intelligence and guidance platform dedicated to helping individuals make informed and confident career decisions. We believe that every person has unique strengths, interests, and potential, and that the right guidance at the right time can transform lives.
Our platform brings together scientifically designed psychometric assessments, career counseling, artificial intelligence, mentorship, internships, competitions, and certification programs into one comprehensive ecosystem. Whether you're a student choosing your first career path, a professional planning a career transition, a parent supporting your child, a teacher guiding learners, or a skill specialist looking to mentor others, Career Garage is built to support your journey.
Our mission is to reduce career confusion by providing personalized insights, practical opportunities, and expert guidance that empower individuals to discover their potential and build successful futures.
At Career Garage, we don't just recommend careers—we help people understand themselves, explore opportunities, develop the right skills, connect with mentors, gain real-world experience, and grow throughout every stage of their career.
We envision a future where every individual has access to reliable career guidance, regardless of their background or location, enabling them to make confident decisions and achieve their full potential.

Introduction
At Career Garage, we believe that Artificial Intelligence (AI) can make career guidance more personalized, accessible, and efficient. However, we also believe that technology should always serve people—not replace their judgment.
This Responsible AI Policy explains how Career Garage uses AI, the principles that guide its development and deployment, and the responsibilities of both Career Garage and our users.
Our AI Philosophy
Artificial Intelligence is designed to assist, inform, and support decision-making—not to make decisions on behalf of individuals.
Our AI systems provide personalized insights and recommendations based on available information, but career decisions remain the responsibility of the individual.
How We Use AI
Career Garage may use AI to assist with:
Career discovery
Career pathway recommendations
Learning pathway suggestions

Personalized learning recommendations
Mentor matching
Internship recommendations
Educational content recommendations
Resume feedback
Interview preparation
Progress tracking
Student engagement
Customer support
Language translation and accessibility features
AI may also help improve platform performance and personalize the user experience.
What AI Does Not Do
Career Garage's AI does not:
Guarantee career success
Guarantee admission to educational institutions
Guarantee employment or internships
Replace professional counselors or mentors
Diagnose psychological or medical conditions
Make legally binding decisions
Make final decisions for users
Our AI is intended to support informed decision-making—not replace human judgment.
Human Oversight
Career Garage believes that important career decisions deserve human understanding.
Where appropriate, AI-generated insights may be supplemented by:
Career counselors
Mentors
Educators
Subject matter experts
Users are encouraged to seek professional advice when making significant educational or career decisions.

1. Human-Centered
People remain at the center of every recommendation.
Technology exists to empower individuals—not control them.
2. Transparency
Whenever AI significantly contributes to recommendations or insights, we aim to make this clear to users.
We strive to explain recommendations in language that is understandable and meaningful.
3. Fairness
We work to reduce unfair bias in our AI systems.
We continually evaluate and improve our models to promote equitable treatment across diverse user groups.
4. Privacy
AI systems only process information in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable laws.
We do not sell personal information.
User data is handled with appropriate security measures.
5. Security
We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect AI systems and user information from unauthorized access or misuse.
6. Continuous Improvement
AI models evolve over time.
We regularly review system performance and improve recommendations based on research, feedback, testing, and technological advancements.
User Responsibilities
Users are encouraged to:
Provide accurate information.
Review AI-generated recommendations thoughtfully.
Consider their own goals, interests, and circumstances.
Consult qualified professionals when appropriate.
Report inaccurate or inappropriate recommendations.
Limitations of AI
Artificial Intelligence has limitations.
Recommendations may be affected by:
Incomplete information
Changes in educational systems
Changes in labor markets
Emerging careers
User-provided data
Technological limitations
AI recommendations should be considered as one source of guidance among many.

Career Garage is committed to developing AI that is:

We seek to continuously improve our systems while respecting the dignity, diversity, and aspirations of every individual.
